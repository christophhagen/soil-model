### Added

- Model definitions with `@Property`, `@Reference` and `@ReferenceList`
- `ModelContext` to create, query, delete and restore model instances
- Property history and read-only snapshots
- `@Query` property wrapper for SwiftUI
- `SynchronousStorage` protocol and `InMemorySynchronousStorage`
